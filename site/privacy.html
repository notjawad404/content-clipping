Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

Clip Studio ("the app", "we") is a desktop tool that turns long videos into short vertical clips and lets the account owner publish them to their own TikTok account. This policy explains what information the app handles when you connect a TikTok account and how it is used.

Information we access

When you choose Connect TikTok and approve access on TikTok's login page, TikTok gives the app permission for these scopes only:

  • user.info.basic: your display name, username and avatar, shown in the app so you can see which account you are posting to.
  • video.upload and video.publish: used to upload a clip you selected and publish it with the title, privacy level and interaction settings you chose.

Before each post the app also reads your posting settings from TikTok (allowed privacy levels, whether comments, Duet and Stitch are available, and the maximum video length) so the posting screen only offers options your account supports.

How we use it

  • To show your TikTok account name and avatar on the posting screen.
  • To upload and publish videos only when you press Post, with the caption and settings you selected.
  • To check the processing status of a post you just made.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling or analytics, and we do not sell, rent or share it with anyone.

Storage and security

The access tokens TikTok issues are stored only on the computer running the app (in a local configuration file) and, if you use scheduled posting, as encrypted secrets in your own GitHub repository. Profile details and posting settings are fetched when needed and are not stored. Video files stay on your computer until you choose to post them; they are then sent directly to TikTok over HTTPS.

Retention and deletion

You can disconnect the app at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions, which revokes its access immediately. Deleting the app's local configuration file removes the stored tokens. Videos you published remain on TikTok and can be deleted there like any other post.

Third parties

The app communicates only with TikTok's official APIs (open.tiktokapis.com). Your use of TikTok is also governed by TikTok's Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children and is not intended for anyone under 18.
