Clip Studio

Clip Studio is a desktop tool for creators. It finds the best moments in a long video, cuts them into vertical clips with captions, and lets you review each clip and publish it to your own TikTok account.

How posting works

  1. Connect your TikTok account with TikTok's official login.
  2. Pick a clip and preview it.
  3. Edit the title, choose who can view it, choose whether others can comment, Duet or Stitch, and disclose branded content if it applies.
  4. Press Post. The clip is uploaded through TikTok's Content Posting API.

Nothing is posted without you pressing Post.
